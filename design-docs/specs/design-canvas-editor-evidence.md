## Platform limitations and recorded deviations

- WebKit clipboard, Japanese IME and touch use synthetic events. They do not establish real system clipboard, keyboard or touch hardware behavior.
- Headless browser audio may remain suspended. In that case audio and A/V sync are unavailable; measured provenance alone is eligible for the sync gate. `estimate` is reported without gating; `unavailable` is a limitation.
- The `window.__vactrPerf.counters().probe` member is currently null. Probe-specific values are therefore unavailable and do not gate this profile.
- Chromium heap is read through CDP after garbage collection. WebKit has no corresponding heap API in this harness and reports the resource ledger only.
- The iPad simulator self-check demonstrates app launch and configuration only. Its performance is not representative of a physical iPad.
- Visual synth outputs use the shipping 640x360 `RENDER_SIZE`, rather than the 15.3.7 product-profile 1024x1024 workload. This is the accepted `L-WORKLOAD-SIZE` deviation.
- Native-tier Hydra is unavailable. Native-tier and iPad video background composition are unavailable because `GlRenderHost` exists only on the browser tier (`editor/src/visual/mount.ts`).
- Audio underrun and drop counters are reported as not exposed when absent from the pinned perf API; the harness does not infer audio-thread independence from UI measurements.
- The host-reported CPAL latency can exclude CoreAudio device or safety-offset latency. Physical acoustic timing is pending.

## Pending physical-iPad procedures

These checks require a physical iPad, the named accessories and a fresh build. Record model, OS, browser/app build, display mode, audio route, capture method, raw files and pass/fail for each procedure. Do not infer a pass from simulator behavior.

| Check | Equipment and exact procedure | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Japanese hardware and software keyboard IME | Physical iPad with Japanese keyboard enabled. In the 20,000-line document, compose and commit Japanese text at the beginning, middle and end; repeat with hardware and software keyboards while audio and visuals run. Record keystrokes and resulting text. | Composition remains visible only in the input bridge, commits exactly once, and leaves document text and selection correct. |
| Touch handles | Physical iPad with touch input. Tap to place the caret, long-press a word, drag both selection handles across Japanese, emoji and a line over 400 columns. | Selection endpoints track the intended grapheme boundaries, text is not duplicated, and handles remain aligned through scroll. |
| VoiceOver | Physical iPad with VoiceOver enabled. Navigate editor, diagnostics, completion popup, transport controls and canvas status using spoken feedback and rotor/keyboard actions. | Source is announced from the accessibility bridge; controls and diagnostics have useful labels; no canvas-only critical action is unreachable. |
| Audio unlock | Fresh app launch with audio route connected. Start playback from the visible Run control and record output plus app logs. | Audio begins only after the user gesture, reports latency provenance, and visual position follows audible output. |
| Interruption and resume | While playing, receive a phone call or invoke Siri, then return to the app. Capture audio and transport epochs before, during and after. | Interruption is reflected; resume establishes a new epoch/correlation; playback does not replay expired highlights or accumulate timing drift. |
| Route change | Repeat playback on wired headphones and Bluetooth. Change route while active and record clock-probe/latency provenance and external loopback audio. | Route change invalidates old correlation, publishes refreshed provenance, and the measured acoustic-to-visual p95 meets 15.3.7 only when uncertainty is at most 10 ms. |
| Orientation | Rotate between portrait and landscape with keyboard shown and hidden while selection and playback are active. | Canvas dimensions, DPR, safe-area/keyboard insets and caret position update without losing document or selection. |
| 120 Hz ProMotion frame time | On a ProMotion iPad, run the product workload with audio, Hydra, scopes and video for five minutes; capture frame timestamps and GPU timing when supported. | Report p50/p95/p99; update/submission p95 <=8 ms and presentation interval p95 <=16.7 ms, p99 <=33.4 ms. |
| Sustained thermal animation | On a physical iPad, run repeated edit/font/DPR/resize cycles with audio and all available visuals for ten minutes. Record temperature/throttling, heap and resource ledger before/after. | No cap is exceeded, retained heap growth is <=8 MiB after warmup/GC, and audio has no UI-stall-attributable underruns. |
| Physical A/V sync | Capture the app display and line/headphone output together with a camera and microphone at the same timebase over five minutes. Repeat per audio route and record correlation uncertainty. | For measured uncertainty <=10 ms, acoustic reference to visible onset p95 <=33.4 ms; no growing offset, and estimated/unavailable routes are reported separately. |
